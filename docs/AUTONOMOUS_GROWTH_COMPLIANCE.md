# Autonomous Growth Compliance

## Required controls

- Use verified public business facts only.
- Never invent relationships, audits, calls, customers, integrations, endorsements, or results.
- Present revenue calculations as illustrative estimates with visible assumptions.
- Suppress contact immediately after an opt-out.
- Stop follow-ups when a person replies.
- Never send to a suppressed destination.
- Maintain source URLs and timestamps for material personalization.
- Keep daily volume, quiet-hour, bounce, complaint, and negative-response thresholds.
- Require approval for pricing changes, discounts, contracts, guarantees, customer evidence, spending, and public complaint responses.

## Prohibited in v1

- Cold SMS
- Automated outbound calls
- Purchased lists
- Repeated test calls
- Fake form submissions
- Call recording without confirmed authorization
- Publicly posting negative prospect audits
- Claims of guaranteed leads, revenue, savings, or business outcomes

## Legal review

The software controls do not replace legal advice. Before production outreach, counsel should review campaign audiences, consent standards, email content, data-source terms, state requirements, telemarketing rules, privacy disclosures, retention, and suppression procedures.
